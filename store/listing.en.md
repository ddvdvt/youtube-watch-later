## Detailed description

With thousands of videos in Watch Later the list stops being useful — you simply stop opening it. This extension helps you sort it out in minutes and brings back a simple thing: "play this next". Works on YouTube in Chrome and Edge (desktop); you need to be signed in.

Watch Later cleanup

• Loads the whole list, not just the first 100 videos, and shows how many are watched, unavailable or Shorts.
• Combinable filters: watched at least N %, deleted and private videos, Shorts, specific channels, duration, "everything except the first K", words in the title. Plus a checkbox on every video.
• Live preview: you see exactly what will be removed.
• The built-in "Remove watched videos" removes every watched video at once. Here you choose, and preview first.

Safe by design

• Dry run — not a single request is sent.
• A backup of the whole list (JSON) is downloaded automatically before anything is removed; a CSV table is one click away.
• Confirmation with the exact number of videos.
• Removal in small batches with pauses; it slows down by itself on "too many requests".
• Pause, cancel, and continue after a page reload.
• Re-add removed videos from a backup.

"Play next" and a queue

• "Next" and "Queue" buttons on video thumbnails, and items in the right-click menu on video links.
• A queue panel on the watch page: drag to reorder, remove, "Next".
• When a video ends, the next one from the queue opens — even with autoplay off.
• Keyboard shortcut Alt+Shift+Q — play the next one now.
• Videos selected in the list can be queued in order, shuffled or from the bottom.

Privacy

No analytics, no ads, no account. No servers of the author: requests go only to www.youtube.com — the same ones the list page itself makes, and only when you ask. Data stays in your browser.

Free and open source (MIT). Interface in Russian and English.
